# G30G — Live Dynamic Surface Validation

## Purpose

Validate the complete WaterLike path under real scene load rather than a
static synthetic deposit:

- bucket outflow and airborne jet;
- high-count impact on the board;
- horizontal film evolution;
- gradual board tilt to 25 degrees;
- sustained downhill runoff;
- moving and oscillating board response;
- multi-camera inspection above and below the board.

The validation used the current SampleScene population of 278,335 particles
and captured 73 four-view frames over 15 simulated seconds.

## Root cause

The gray granular/checkerboard pattern was generated by an unstable explicit
film-evolution step, not by texture conflicts or deposited particle colors.

Capillary diffusion and surface advection were previously combined in one
update. At low frame rates, the large delta time exceeded the positivity
limit. Negative cells were clipped to zero while positive cells continued to
grow, creating both the visible checkerboard and artificial paint volume.
Open-edge outflow was also subtracted twice.

## Production changes

- Bounded capillary response below the four-neighbour stability limit.
- Split capillary diffusion and advection into separate conservative GPU
  passes.
- Limited advection to a positivity-preserving per-pass CFL budget.
- Added one to five adaptive advection substeps only when projected surface
  acceleration requires them.
- Removed duplicate edge-outflow subtraction.
- Changed particle visualization from octahedra to camera-facing splats.
- Made splat orientation derive from each camera's inverse view matrix, which
  supports validation cameras, multi-camera rendering, and VR correctly.

## Measured result

At the end of the full moving-board run:

- deposited particle volume: `0.0225431889 m3`;
- board film volume: `0.0225424274 m3`;
- film/deposited ratio: `0.9999662`;
- relative volume error: approximately `0.0034%`;
- WaterLike downhill centroid travel: approximately `53` cells, or `0.55 m`;
- particles detected below the board at the final sample: `2 / 278335`.

The granular checkerboard is absent from the final frame sequence.

## Remaining visual-physics item

The two jets remain coherent near their holes but become broad particle sprays
after leaving the short MLS-MPM jet collar. Long-range jet cohesion or a
surface-reconstructed fluid renderer should be handled as a separate stage;
it is not part of the film-instability fix.
